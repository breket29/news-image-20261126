A program that sums up the world news of Thursday, 26 November 2026 in one dense front-page image: Trump's sweeping new tariffs on 14 trading partners under Section 301 and the market slide that followed, Russian ballistic-missile strikes on Kyiv, Ukrainian strikes on the Savasleyka air base, and 40 more verified dated stories across nine regions — from the Macy's parade postponement to North Korea's call for wider tactical-nuclear deployment. It validates the poster PNG and writes it to /output/entry.png.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The poster itself is drawn by `draw_poster.py` (PIL) and copied from the repository by `main.py`.
